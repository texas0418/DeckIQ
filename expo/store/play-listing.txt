FULL DESCRIPTION (4000 chars max)
-----------------------------------------------------
Type a topic. Get a deck. Start studying.

DeckIQ turns anything you need to learn — "photosynthesis," "Spanish past tense," "aviation weather minimums," your own pasted notes — into clean, study-ready flashcards in seconds. No more spending the whole evening MAKING the cards instead of learning them.

AI THAT DOES THE BUSYWORK
• Enter any topic and DeckIQ generates a focused deck instantly
• Cards come out clear and quizzable — terms, definitions, the questions an instructor would actually ask
• Regenerate or trim until the deck is exactly what you need

STUDY SESSIONS THAT STICK
• Flip through decks in a clean, distraction-free study view
• Track progress and mastery per deck — know what's solid and what needs another pass
• Organized by category, from quick-browse collections to your own custom decks

MADE FOR REAL STUDYING
Students, professionals prepping for certifications, language learners, trivia obsessives — if you'd study it, DeckIQ will card it.

Simple, fast, and focused: open the app, name the thing you need to know, and get to work.
